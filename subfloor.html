Play with the mouse, touch, or your keyboard: A–; for notes, W E T Y U O P for sharps, Z/X to shift octave. Hold several keys and let go to hear the glide. With the arpeggiator on, hold a chord (or a single note with 2–3 octaves) and it steps through the notes in time; turn on Hold and tap keys to add or remove them from the pattern. Gate at 100% ties the steps together so glide slides between them. Most laptop and phone speakers can't reproduce anything under about 60 Hz, so use headphones or monitors for the sub range.
